Common Questions

Q: I'm not technical. Is this too advanced?
No. This course is designed for pharma data professionals. While basic familiarity with data or programming helps, we focus on mindset and architecture, not writing complex software code from scratch. We explain MCP, RAG, and Multi-Agent in pharma language, focusing specifically on the agent skills that has the most potential to amplify your existing domain expertise.
Q: My company has compliance restrictions on AI tools. Will this apply?
Yes. We specifically discuss "agentic AI in regulated industries". You do not need to use your company's data or infrastructure. We guide you finding public-domain datasets and documents. Experient in personal environments so you can build your skills freely before bringing them to work.
Q: I'm too busy for a long course.
6 weeks, self-paced, 3-4 hours per week. Designed for 40+ hour work weeks. No required live sessions.
Q: How is this different from free tutorials?

Q: Will others actually care about this?
People care about results. When you can show an SDTM validation agent skill you built, or a system that saved your team 10 hours a week—that matters far more than any certificate.
Q: What if I don't finish?
